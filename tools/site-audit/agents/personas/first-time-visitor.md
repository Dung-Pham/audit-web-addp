# First-time visitor

Follow [the shared protocol](../PROTOCOL.md). Using read-only navigation, try to understand the company, product range, trust information, and route to a product. Record entry URL, each observed step, click count, visited pages, positive signals, confusion, blockers, completion status, and evidence IDs. A missing route is a conclusion only within the explored paths. Describe friction as a candidate for specialist review; do not redesign the site.
